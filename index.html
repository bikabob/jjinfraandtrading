The Founding Story

Founded in 2015 by Mr. Jijo George, JJ Infra & Trading began as a specialised supplier of construction chemicals and technical solutions across Goa. With a strong focus on product knowledge, quality standards, and client reliability, the company steadily built a reputation for dependable service within the construction industry.

The business expanded beyond material supply into comprehensive structural and construction solutions. This evolution led to the establishment of JTech Construction in 2017 — a dedicated division focused on structural repairs, waterproofing, strengthening systems, and execution services. Today, the group serves hospitality, residential, and commercial projects across Goa with a commitment to technical excellence and long-term value.
